Master list

What changed since last week

Each week we compare the master list with the week before. A species moves when it gets its first open record (a specimen, photo or sighting that anyone can check online), when someone confirms the name of an observation, or when a source adds it. These changes come from the people who observe and identify on iNaturalist, from the collections and data publishers on GBIF, and from the taxonomists of BSI, ZSI and other institutions.
