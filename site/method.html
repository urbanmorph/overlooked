Sources and method

Each month a scheduled job takes fresh data from the sources, computes every figure again, and publishes the result as a dated snapshot. Nobody edits the figures by hand. A snapshot never changes after it is published.

The monthly run

  1. 01Check the sourcesOne request to each source. If GBIF or the data store does not answer, the run stops.
  2. 02DownloadThree GBIF downloads, one after another. Each has its own DOI.
  3. 03PlaceEach record goes to its LGD district by its coordinates.
  4. 04ComputeThe ledger, the groups, and the records with no species name.
  5. 05CheckEvery download has a DOI. Large changes from last month stop the run.
  6. 06PublishThe files go to the data store. The site reads them in your browser.

The job can run again safely. It does not repeat finished steps, and it does not send a file again if the data store already has the same file. If GBIF reports no change since the last run, the month downloads nothing.

What the words mean

Public record
A record in GBIF with a place in India and a date. Specimens, observations and records from papers all count.
No public record since 1980
GBIF has no record of the species in the area after 1980. It does not mean that the species is not there now. Records can exist that nobody has published.
Quiet
A species with records in an area, but no record after the year that you select. The default year is 1980.
Group not recorded since the year
If a group has no record in an area after the year, Overlooked does not count that group as quiet there. Possibly nobody surveyed the group.
District
LGD district boundaries. A record goes to a district by its coordinates, rounded to 3 decimals (about 110 m). A point less than 5 km from land goes to the nearest district. A point farther out is offshore and is not in the ledger.
Waiting for a name
An iNaturalist observation that does not have an agreed species name yet, or a GBIF record that has only a genus, family or higher name.

Records used

  • Country: India, with coordinates and no known problem with the place
  • Status: present
  • Uncertainty of the place: 25 km or less
  • No fossils, and no living specimens in zoos or gardens
  • Some datasets are not used, for example gene-bank and compiled crop data

Names follow the Catalogue of Life, as GBIF uses it. Species links go to the Catalogue of Life.
