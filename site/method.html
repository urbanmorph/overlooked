Sources and method

Each month a scheduled job takes fresh data from the sources, computes every figure again, and publishes the result as a dated snapshot. Nobody edits the figures by hand. A snapshot never changes after it is published.

The monthly run

  1. 01Check the sourcesOne request to each source. If GBIF or the data store does not answer, the run stops.
  2. 02DownloadGBIF downloads, one after another, each with its own DOI. iNaturalist species lists per state. The checklists and the IUCN Red List when they change.
  3. 03PlaceEach record goes to its LGD district by its coordinates.
  4. 04ComputeThe master list: every species from every source, with what is observed, what is identified, and the IUCN status, for India and each state.
  5. 05CheckEvery download has a DOI. Large changes from last month stop the run.
  6. 06PublishThe files go to the data store. The site reads them in your browser.

The job can run again safely. It does not repeat finished steps, and it does not send a file again if the data store already has the same file. If GBIF reports no change since the last run, the month downloads nothing.

What the words mean

Documented
At least one source lists the species for India: a ZSI or BSI checklist, Kew's plant list, GBIF, iNaturalist, Plazi or the IUCN Red List. A listing by BSI or ZSI counts.
Observed
At least one observation of the species in India: a photo, a sighting, a specimen, a sound, a DNA sample, or a record from a paper, in GBIF or iNaturalist.
Identified
The name is confirmed: an iNaturalist research-grade observation, or a GBIF record named to species.
Described
The year the species was first described. For a species described from India since 2007: the year of the paper (Plazi, BSI Plant Discoveries, ZSI Animal Discoveries). Else the year in the Catalogue of Life author citation, else in the ZSI checklist, else in Kew's plant list (for a plant renamed later, the year of its first name). Most fungi, lichens and mosses have no year in these sources.
No record since it was described
No GBIF record of the species from India after the year of its description. This is checked only for species described since 2007 that the yearly BSI Plant Discoveries and ZSI Animal Discoveries editions or Plazi list. For a species that an edition lists in a later year than its description (for example a new record for India), it is not checked.
IUCN status
The category on the IUCN Red List (CR, EN, VU, NT, LC, DD), or not assessed. Threatened means CR, EN or VU.
Waiting for a name
An iNaturalist observation with no agreed name yet. The observer may have named it to species, to genus only, or only to a family or a higher level.
Name not matched
A source name that does not match a Catalogue of Life name. It stays on the list under its own name, with this label.
District and state
LGD boundaries. A GBIF record goes to a district by its coordinates, rounded to 3 decimals (about 110 m). A point less than 5 km from land goes to the nearest district. A record that cannot go in a district goes to a state when its state name is clear, else it counts only for India. These records have no coordinates, coordinates that are uncertain by more than 25 km, or coordinates that GBIF marks as possibly wrong. GBIF records with coordinates outside India are not used. DNA sequences with no coordinates are not used, because their dates are often the date the sequence was sent.
No exact location
The species is observed in India, but no record of it can go in a district (see District and state).
Recorded under the name as published
GBIF has records of the species from India after its description, but only under the name as the paper gives it: GBIF has not added the name to its species list yet. The species counts as observed in India, in no state.

Records used

  • Country: India, with coordinates and no known problem with the place
  • Status: present
  • Uncertainty of the place: 25 km or less
  • No fossils, and no living specimens in zoos or gardens
  • Some datasets are not used, for example gene-bank and compiled crop data
  • DNA sequence records count. A species known only from DNA sequences has the label "DNA only". Host-organism records from sequence archives are not used

Names follow the Catalogue of Life, as GBIF uses it. Species links go to the Catalogue of Life.
