Records to fix

For data publishers. GBIF has these records from India, but this site cannot use all of them: some have no exact location, and some have no species name. Correct them in your dataset. The next weekly update then uses them, and the species move closer to the right end of the bar on the home page.

Names to check

For people who know a group. No Indian checklist lists these species. They are on our India list only because GBIF matched records from India under an older name, and the Catalogue of Life puts that name under them. A related species is on an Indian checklist, so the records may be that species. For example, records of the Indian flying fox under its old name Pteropus giganteus went to Pteropus vampyrus of Southeast Asia; we now correct that. The strongest come first: an older name that a checklist uses for another species, then a species whose IUCN Red List range map does not include India.
