Follow-up on new species

Scientists describe hundreds of new species from India each year. For each one, this page asks: did anyone observe it again after the description, is the name confirmed, and what is its IUCN status?

The first results are on their way

Overlooked collects the names and asks GBIF for the records. This takes some hours. Then this page shows the results.

How a follow-up is found

  1. The name as published, in this month's GBIF data.
  2. GBIF's match against the Catalogue of Life, which also finds synonyms.
  3. GBIF records under the name as published, when GBIF could not match it to a species.

"Observed again" means a record dated after the year of the description. Records copied from papers (Plazi) count only as "later papers". Records from the year of the description are not counted, because they are often the type material. "Identified" and "IUCN" come from the master list, as on the rest of the site.

Where the names come from

Plazi: species with an Indian holotype, marked "sp. nov." (CC0). Botanical Survey of India: Plant Discoveries, species new to science. Zoological Survey of India: Animal Discoveries, species new to science. BSI and ZSI material is reproduced with acknowledgement. Journals that Plazi does not process are missing, for example most issues of the Journal of Threatened Taxa after 2020.
