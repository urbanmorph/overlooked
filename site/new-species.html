Follow-up on new species

Scientists describe hundreds of new species from India each year. This page asks one question about each species described since 2015: is there a public record of it after the description?

The first results are on their way

Overlooked collects the names and asks GBIF for the records. This takes some hours. Then this page shows the results.

How a follow-up is found

  1. The name as published, in this month's snapshot.
  2. GBIF's match against the Catalogue of Life, which also finds synonyms.
  3. GBIF records under the name as published, when GBIF could not match it to a species.

A follow-up is a record dated after the year of the description. Records copied from papers (Plazi) count only as "later papers". Records from the year of the description are not counted, because they are often the type material.

Where the names come from

Plazi: species with an Indian holotype, marked "sp. nov." (CC0). Botanical Survey of India: Plant Discoveries, species new to science (reproduced with acknowledgement). Journals that Plazi does not process are missing, for example most issues of the Journal of Threatened Taxa after 2020.
