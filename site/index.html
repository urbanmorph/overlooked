Wait for a name:

Observations on iNaturalist that the observer named to species and that nobody has confirmed yet. Species at the top matter most: no identification here yet, threatened, or endemic. Open them on iNaturalist to agree or to give a better name (you need an iNaturalist account).

Observations on iNaturalist named only to genus. A person who knows the genus can often name the species. Genera at the top matter most: not on the India list yet (a possible new record for India), or with species that nobody has identified in India.

Observations on iNaturalist named only to a family, an order or a higher level. A person who knows the group can often name the genus or the species. Each link opens the observations of one group.
